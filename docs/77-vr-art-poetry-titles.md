# 77 — VR titles about visual artists/writers, Dalí-adjacent, for the demo booth

> Desk research, 2026-08-26, web search only (Steam store pages, UploadVR/RoadToVR/6DoF
> Reviews, XRMust). Not run on the headset. Goal: more first-timer-friendly booth pieces like
> "Dreams of Dalí" (appid 591360) — minimal input, strong visual hook, short sessions fine.

## 1. Banksy — still not purchasable, confirmed

No purchasable or downloadable Banksy VR title exists on Steam, Meta Quest store, Viveport,
or itch.io as of this search. Two things exist and neither counts:

- **"Banksy: The Walled Off Hotel VR"** (dir. Amer Shomali, funded by Saudi Arabia's Red Sea
  Fund, announced 2022) is explicitly **unofficial** — Banksy's own representative said so on
  the record (Variety, 2022). It's in the XRMust festival database (20 min) with no
  purchase/download link — festival-circuit-only, not a shipped consumer title. No
  release-to-store news for 2025/2026 despite the physical hotel reopening in Bethlehem in
  December 2025.
- **"On Location with Banksy in VR"** was an AltspaceVR room. AltspaceVR was shut down by
  Microsoft in March 2023; it no longer exists anywhere.

Verdict: keep telling people there's no Banksy VR piece to buy. Nothing changed.

## 2. Other artist/writer titles that ARE real and available today

All checked directly against their store pages (linked below) on 2026-08-26.

**The Night Cafe: A VR Tribute to Vincent van Gogh** — Steam appid 482390, **free**. Walk
through a recreation of Van Gogh's painting and other works. SteamVR, "Tracked Controller
Support" (grab/point with a controller, standing/room-scale, not gaze-only) — simpler than a
full game but not as hands-off as Dalí's gaze-dwell. 95% positive over 271 reviews. Should
run like any other SteamVR title on this rig, through xrizer against Monado — untested on
this headset specifically. store.steampowered.com/app/482390

**Anne Frank House VR** — Steam appid 2877690, **free**. A writer (diarist), not a painter,
but the same "walk through a real place and absorb it" shape as Dalí. Listed for SteamVR,
Quest, Rift/Rift S, Valve Index, Vive/Vive Pro. Input is "Tracked Controller Support" —
point-and-click on floor markers to move, touch doorknobs/objects with a virtual hand; no
twin-stick or precision aiming. ~25 minutes, narrated in 7 languages. Same SteamVR-via-xrizer
caveat as above. store.steampowered.com/app/2877690

**The Scream** — Steam appid 1097120, **$3.99**. A documentary-style piece built around
Munch's painting, three chapters. SteamVR or Oculus PC, "Tracked Controller Support" plus
optional Leap Motion hand tracking, standing or room-scale. Controller-based, not gaze-only,
but framed as a passive documentary walk rather than a game — likely low input load in
practice, unverified. store.steampowered.com/app/1097120

## 3. Found but NOT recommending (real, but wrong fit or wrong platform)

- **Verse VR** (Steam appid 2958710, $4.49, poetry/folk-song/art) — the Steam page only lists
  **Meta Quest 3S/3/Pro/2** as supported hardware, no SteamVR/PCVR headset. Quest-hardware-bound
  regardless of OS; won't work with a G2. Input is pointing+trigger on glowing orbs (UploadVR).
- **Notes on Blindness: Into Darkness** — real, award-winning (XR Peabody 2021), but
  distributed via Oculus/Gear VR and Quest App Lab/SideQuest, not Steam. No PCVR/SteamVR build
  found; Quest-exclusive in practice.
- **Frida Kahlo VR** — every hit is a **location-based touring exhibit** (Salt Lake City, DC,
  Albuquerque, Brussels) handing out Quest headsets on-site, not a downloadable/purchasable app.
- **Munch VR** (Steam appid 549000) is an unrelated underwater game sharing the painter's
  name — not an art piece, don't confuse with "The Scream" above.

## Bottom line

Add **The Night Cafe** and **Anne Frank House VR** to the booth shortlist (both free, both
SteamVR, both point/touch input, no aiming or locomotion sticks) for a trial run alongside
Dalí; **The Scream** as a paid backup if the free two run well. Banksy stays a documented
dead end, not a gap in the search.
